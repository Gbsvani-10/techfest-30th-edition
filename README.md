# TECHFEST — 30th Edition | IIT Bombay

A futuristic and immersive landing page concept celebrating the **30th Edition of Techfest, IIT Bombay**, scheduled for December 16–18, 2026.

Designed with a bold visual identity inspired by industrial architecture, advanced engineering, robotics and next-generation technology.

## ✨ Overview

This project reimagines the Techfest experience through a cinematic, modern and interactive web interface. It combines immersive visuals, smooth animations, responsive layouts and engaging storytelling to showcase the spirit of one of Asia's largest science and technology festivals.

## 🚀 Features

- **Cinematic Hero Section** — Bold typography, dramatic visuals and immersive backgrounds.
- **About Techfest** — Highlights the festival's history and 30th Edition.
- **Programs & Competitions** — Showcases robotics, Robowars, AI challenges and workshops.
- **Campus Experiences** — Features global exhibitions, keynote lectures and Technoholix.
- **Interactive Gallery** — Visual exploration of robotics, exhibitions and stage experiences.
- **Live Countdown** — Countdown to December 16, 2026.
- **Festival Timeline** — Interactive three-day event overview.
- **Smooth Animations** — Scroll-triggered transitions, parallax effects and interactive elements.
- **Responsive Design** — Optimized for desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The visual direction combines:

- Deep black and industrial steel aesthetics.
- Electric orange and crimson accents.
- Oversized editorial typography.
- Cinematic imagery and layered compositions.
- Subtle motion and interactive transitions.

The goal is to create a premium digital experience that reflects innovation, engineering excellence and the energy of Techfest.

